Founded in 1972, PATH Employment Services is an award-winning, non-profit agency connecting Hamilton residents with meaningful work. We were the first community agency in Canada to receive dedicated funding to support People with Disabilities in employment, and that legacy of breaking down barriers shapes everything we do. Today, we support jobseekers from all walks of life, with a specialization in disability-inclusive hiring.

Our team of specialists covers every aspect of employment support: career development, job matching, employer partnerships, and more. We're funded by the Government of Canada and the Province of Ontario, which means every single one of our services is available at no cost to jobseekers and the employers who hire them. Real support. Real jobs. Real results.

We work with two groups, and our support for each is tailored, personal, and at no cost to you.

For Employers
  • Individual support to understand your hiring needs and goals
  • A dedicated point of contact who gets to know your business over time
  • Access to personalized one-on-one or group hiring events and job fairs
  • Use of our office space to hold job fairs and interviews
  • Pre-screening of candidates so you only meet people who are genuinely ready
  • Accessibility and inclusion best practices support
  • Access to PATH's social media channels to promote your job postings to a wider community audience
For Jobseekers
  • A dedicated Career Development Specialist (CDS) assigned to you
  • Career exploration and direction-setting
  • Resume and cover letter writing
  • Effective job search strategies
  • Training and skills development, including our Power of Possible Job Readiness Program
  • Disability disclosure and accommodation support
  • Interview preparation and practice
  • Referrals to community services and programs
Support continues for up to 1 year after you start working, because keeping a job matters just as much as finding one.

Our main office is at Suite 501, 12 James Street North, Hamilton, Ontario L8R 2J9, and we serve the Hamilton area and surrounding communities.

To reach our main office, call 905-528-6611, online at pathemployment.com, or by email at reception@pathemployment.com. Office hours are Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

Yes, there is absolutely no cost. Because we're funded by the Government of Canada and the Province of Ontario, there is never a cost to the jobseekers we support or to the employers we partner with. No catch. No invoices. No hidden fees.

We're here because we believe meaningful employment should be accessible for all, and we work hard to make sure cost is never a barrier.

For BIA Member Employers

What PATH Can Do for Your Business

We're not a job board. We're a hiring partner: we do the legwork so you can focus on running your business. Everything below is at no cost to you.

🔍

Pre-Screened Candidates

We get to know your business, your team, and exactly what you're looking for, then we pre-screen jobseekers before they ever reach your desk. No sifting through stacks of resumes. Just qualified people who fit.

🎪

Job Fairs & Hiring Events

PATH coordinates multiple job fairs and hiring events throughout the year, including large-scale events across Hamilton. We can work with your BIA to organize events that bring the right candidates directly to your members.

♿

Accessibility & Inclusion Support

Our team provides Accessibility and Inclusion support: helping your business attract a wider talent pool, meet accessibility standards, and build a workplace where everyone can do their best work.

📅

In-Work Support Team

Once someone is hired, our In-Work Support Team stays connected to help new employees settle in, build confidence, and succeed in their role long-term.

How We Can Work Together

Engagement Ideas for Hamilton's BIAs

We're not here to drop off a brochure and disappear. We want to show up for BIA leadership, for member businesses, and for the people who live in each neighbourhood.

Getting to Know You

Drop-In BIA Visits

We'd love to visit each BIA individually to learn what makes your area unique, understand the employment landscape in your neighbourhood, and hear directly from your members about what they need. No agenda, no pitch. Just a real conversation.

Community Outreach

Pop-Up Employment Support Events

We can bring our services right into your neighbourhood: setting up pop-up tables or resource booths where local residents can walk up for employment information, resume reviews, or career assessments. Quick, accessible, and at no cost to your community.

Employer Partnerships

Tailored Hiring Support

For BIA member businesses that are actively hiring, we'll sit down with them directly: learn their goals, understand their challenges, and build a hiring strategy together. We match. We screen. We support. And it costs nothing.

Spotlighting Your Community

BIA Social Media Campaign

We'd love to highlight each BIA through our social media channels: showcasing the businesses, the community, and the people that make each area what it is. A chance to tell your story to a wider audience while connecting residents to the supports they need.

For Jobseekers in Your Community

Support for the People Who Live Here

Every BIA is a neighbourhood, and the people who live there deserve real employment support. Here's what PATH offers individuals, at no cost.

Resume writing & cover letters: personalized, professional, and ready to use

Interview preparation: practice, feedback, and confidence-building

Job search strategies: practical, tailored guidance for today's market

Career assessments: helping people figure out what direction is right for them

Disability & accommodation support: disclosure guidance, workplace accommodations, and assistive technology

Workshops: covering everything from LinkedIn to workplace readiness

Funding for employment needs: in some cases, help covering work boots, transit, or a phone

Power of Possible (POP): PATH's Job Readiness Program, building the skills, confidence, and tools jobseekers need to succeed in the workplace

Employer connections: direct introductions to hiring businesses across Hamilton

Anyone can walk through our doors. Self-referrals are always welcome, and ODSP recipients can also be referred directly. Getting started is as simple as a phone call to 905-528-6611.
